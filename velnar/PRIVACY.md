# NEXORA privacy policy

NEXORA changes the appearance of pages on `github.com`. The extension uses access to that site to apply styles and identify the current repository when you preview a theme or choose a repository-specific palette.

NEXORA does not transmit data to its developer or any third party. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or access to GitHub credentials or tokens. The extension stores preferences, custom CSS, saved themes, and associated repository names on your device through browser extension storage. The files you explicitly export are saved to your device by your browser.

To remove stored preferences, use **Settings → Reset All Settings** in the extension or remove the extension through your browser. You can remove exported files separately.

For privacy questions, contact the project through [GitHub](https://github.com/MaJiD-PiXeL/NEXORA_Project/issues).
