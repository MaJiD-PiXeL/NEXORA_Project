NEXORA does not replace your profile or ask for your account. It gives the interface you see every day a visual system that belongs to you.

  1. 01

    Explore the worlds

    Preview every cinematic theme and palette before applying anything.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your GitHub data
doesn’t leave your browser.

NEXORA has no sign-in, no analytics, no remote artwork, and no GitHub token requirement. Themes, preferences, and custom CSS are stored locally through browser storage.
